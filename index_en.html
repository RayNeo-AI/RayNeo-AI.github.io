/ Research Directions

Overview

Our research aims to bridge the gap between the physical world and digital intelligence. By building Egocentric Multimodal Large Models, we empower smart glasses to "understand" the world; through Efficient On-Device Training & Inference, we ensure intelligence is accessible and privacy-secure; by exploring Implicit Knowledge, we enable AI to possess intuition and common sense similar to humans.

Egocentric Understanding

  • ● Achievement 1: Efficient spatiotemporal modeling for egocentric video.
  • ● Achievement 2: Intent recognition for dynamic object interaction in complex scenes.
  • ● Achievement 3: EgoThink: Evaluating the first-person thinking capability of multimodal large models.

On-Device Training

  • ● Achievement 1: On-device LLM training architecture under extremely low power consumption.
  • ● Achievement 2: Model quantization and compression techniques tailored for smart glasses.
  • ● Achievement 3: Cloud-Edge collaborative continuous learning mechanisms.

Implicit Knowledge

  • ● Achievement 1: Mechanism analysis and suppression methods for large model hallucinations.
  • ● Achievement 2: Extracting commonsense knowledge graphs from unstructured data.
  • ● Achievement 3: Research on rapid inference paths simulating human intuition.
